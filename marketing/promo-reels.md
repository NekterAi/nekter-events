## Boost playbook

### Which to boost
- **Primary spend:** Reel 1 and Reel 2 (strongest hooks, broadest pain).
- **Secondary:** Reel 4 (thesis) once 1 and 2 have data.
- **Retargeting only:** Reel 5 (urgency) to warm audiences.

### Objective
Boost for **"More website visits"** (link clicks to event.nekter.ai), NOT
"More profile visits" or "More engagement." You want registrations, so optimize
for the click. If the post performs organically first (good save/share rate),
boost the winner rather than guessing.

### Audience
- **Location:** start Las Vegas + 25mi (you can mention local), then expand to
  US-wide if it converts. For guerrilla/local synergy, keep a Vegas-tight set
  running alongside.
- **Age:** 30 to 55.
- **Interests:** small business owner, entrepreneurship, Shopify, real estate,
  HVAC/home services, marketing agency, GoHighLevel, HubSpot, ChatGPT, Claude.
  Layer "business owner" behaviors if available.
- **Gender:** all.

### Budget
- Start **$10 to $20/day per reel** for 3 to 4 days. Kill anything under a 1%
  click rate. Pour budget into the winner.
- Total test budget across 2 reels: ~$120 to $160 over 4 days. Scale the winner
  to $30 to $50/day for the final run-up to June 25.

### Creative rules that get boosts APPROVED + performing
- Captions burned in (80% watch on mute).
- Hook in the first 2 seconds, no slow intro, no logo splash.
- Keep medical/financial "income claims" out. "Find $10k-$50k" is fine on the
  landing page; in a boosted ad it can trip Meta's review. On the reel, sell the
  outcome (build your own AI assistant), not a revenue promise.
- One CTA. "Link in bio" organically; the boost itself gets a "Sign Up" / "Learn
  More" button pointing to event.nekter.ai.

### Tracking
- Use a UTM on the boost link: event.nekter.ai/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=workshop-june25
  (the form already tags everyone source=webinar in the CRM, so you'll see IG
  traffic land as webinar leads; the UTM lets you confirm paid vs organic in
  analytics).

### Cadence to June 25
- ~16 days out: post Reel 1 + Reel 2 organically, boost the better one.
- ~12 days out: Reel 4, boost.
- ~7 days out: Reel 3 organic, start Reel 5 retargeting boost.
- Final 3 days: scale the winning reel, push urgency in stories with the
  registration link sticker.
