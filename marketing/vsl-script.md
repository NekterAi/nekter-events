# VSL Script — June 25 Workshop (90 seconds)

For the hero video on event.nekter.ai. Spencer to camera. Matches the
**5-skills** curriculum (not the old 5-tools framing). Voice: operator, direct,
specific numbers, no buzzwords, no em-dashes.

**Run time:** ~90 seconds. **Setup:** lavalier + phone/camera, eye-level, clean
background (or the Higgsfield/Sora background swap if you want the cinematic look).
Teleprompter optional, but better if it feels off-the-cuff.

---

## The script

**[0:00 — Hook, look right into the lens]**

"If you run a business and you keep hearing you should be using AI, but every
time you try it just hands you generic garbage, this is for you. I'm Spencer,
I run an AI company, and I'm going to show you the five skills that actually make
AI do real work."

**[0:12 — The problem]**

"Here's what nobody tells you. It's not about which tool you use. Everyone's out
here collecting AI apps like Pokemon cards. The people actually getting leverage
learned a handful of skills, and those skills work in the tools you already have."

**[0:28 — The five, fast]**

"On June 25, live, I'll walk you through all five.

One, prompting that actually gets usable output the first time.

Two, Claude Projects, so everything it writes sounds like you, not a robot.

Three, connectors, so AI plugs into your Gmail, your calendar, your docs, and
actually does the work instead of just talking about it.

Four, skills, turning one-off prompts into workflows you reuse every week.

And five, you'll build your own custom GPT, trained on your business, live, in
the session."

**[0:58 — The wedge / custom-GPT angle]**

"That last one matters. People are charging four figures for a custom GPT of
their own playbook. You don't need to buy theirs. I'll show you how to build
your own in a few minutes, and you walk out with it. It's yours."

**[1:12 — What they leave with]**

"This isn't a lecture. You'll open the tools, build alongside me, and leave with
a working custom GPT and the prompts to keep going. Sixty minutes. It's free."

**[1:24 — CTA]**

"Save your seat for June 25 right below this video. I'll see you live."

**[end ~1:30]**

---

## Delivery notes

- **Hook is everything.** First 5 seconds decide if they watch. Say the hook
  with energy, then settle into a calmer pace.
- **Slow down on the five.** Don't rush the list, it's the spine. A beat between
  each number.
- **The custom-GPT line is the emotional peak.** That's where you sound like an
  operator calling out the gurus. Lean in.
- **Numbers land.** "Four figures", "a few minutes", "sixty minutes", "free".
- **Don't name names.** No Cardone, no specific guru. "People are charging" keeps
  it clean and keeps Jodie out of the crossfire.
- **End looking into the lens** on "see you live."
